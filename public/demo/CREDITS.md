# Sample book credits

`the-time-machine.epub` is *The Time Machine* by H. G. Wells (1895), from
[Project Gutenberg eBook #35](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/35).

The text is in the public domain in the United States and in countries where copyright
lasts for the life of the author plus 70 years (Wells died in 1946).

It is bundled only so the reading guide can be tried without supplying a book first.
Book Tutor does not ship any other book, and the app never uploads one.
